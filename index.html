Proximater FAQs

What is a broadcast?

A broadcast is essentially like a business (or social) listing with a single picture taken directly from your camera, up to 5 tags to describe it, along with your location. The broadcast can be a maximum length of 10 hours to keep information fresh. Your location is placed on a map, and a card-type view is used in a list for when users pick up the app to see what is nearby.

How do I create a user account

Proximater is free to play so you do not need a user account to see what is around you!

If you wish to create a broadcast or navigate then you will need to sign in using your Apple or Google account or create a user account with your email address.

How are you protecting my sensitive data?

Proximater has been built from the ground up around the best information security practices available for mobile apps. We were able to do this by tapping into the years of experience that our team has ascertained from delivering mobile apps for corporate and consumer household names.

By working towards ISO-27001 certification, we have an ongoing commitment to ensure that security is managed, and maintained.

How can I download Proximater?

Proximater is available to download now from the Apple App Store, and Google Play Store, find download links at the bottom of the page.

How does Proximater protect my privacy?

You can use Proximater to share your location. On top of the strictest operating-system level permissions, through clear messaging and embedded features you always know where you are with your privacy.

How is Proximater being socially responsible?
Inappropriate content

We do not wish for content to appear on Proximater that may be deemed offensive or inappropriate for minors.

To tackle this from launch we have almighty OpenAI, and AWS AI integrations, which are doing a great job at blocking inappropriate content.

The environment

We are determined to find concrete solutions using the latest technology to support biodiversity and the environment. Proximater will hopefully help with that.
